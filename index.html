FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is Fixtock for?

Retailers, wholesalers, pharmacies, supermarkets, distributors and any business that buys, stores and sells goods, anywhere in the world, from a single shop to several locations with a team.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets, plus any modern browser through the web app. Sign in with the same account everywhere and your data stays in sync.

Can I manage more than one store?

Yes. Add your locations, move stock between them and see figures per store or for the whole business. How many stores you can add depends on your plan.

Does it work in my country and currency?

Yes. Fixtock is available in 178 countries and regions, and works with every world currency. Each company chooses its own currency and time zone, and every figure, report and receipt uses them.

Can my staff use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Invite each person with a role. Viewers can only look, while Editors and Admins get more access. You stay in control as the Owner, and every action is recorded.

What happens if the internet drops?

Fixtock keeps showing your most recent figures and tells you they may be out of date, then refreshes as soon as you’re back online.

Who owns my data?

Your business does. We use it only to run Fixtock for you, never sell it, and you can ask for it to be deleted. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

How much does it cost?

There’s a free plan to get started, and paid plans (Lite, Pro and Enterprise) for more stores, more stock items and advanced features. Pay through the App Store, Google Play or by card on the web, and cancel any time.
