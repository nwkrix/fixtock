FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is Fixtock for?

Retailers, wholesalers, pharmacies, supermarkets, distributors and any business that buys, stores and sells goods, from a single shop to several locations with a team.

Can I manage more than one store?

Yes. Add your locations, move stock between them and see figures per store or for the whole business. How many stores you can add depends on your plan.

Does it work in my currency?

Yes. Each company chooses its own currency and time zone, and every figure, report and receipt uses them.

Can my staff use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Invite each person with a role. Viewers can only look, while Editors and Admins get more access. You stay in control as the Owner, and every action is recorded.

Who owns my data?

Your business does. We use it only to run Fixtock for you, never sell it, and you can ask for it to be deleted. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

How much does it cost?

There's a free plan to get started, and paid plans (Lite, Pro and Enterprise) for more stores, more stock items and advanced features. Paid plans are billed through the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel any time.

When is it available?

Fixtock is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Email us and we'll let you know the day it launches.
